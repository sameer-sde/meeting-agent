Meeting Agent

Skip the call.
Keep the decisions.

A bot joins your Teams and Google Meet calls, understands almost any language, even mixed ones, and sends you who promised what, even when you weren't there.

Why we built this

Every meeting matters. Not every meeting fits your day.

Calendars collide, priorities shift, and the most important conversations often take place when you are needed elsewhere. What follows is all too familiar: scattered notes, second-hand summaries, and decisions nobody quite remembers making.

Meeting Agent attends on your behalf. It captures the entire conversation and delivers a precise, structured record: the decisions taken, the commitments made, and who is accountable for each.

Be absent from the meeting. Never from the outcome.
