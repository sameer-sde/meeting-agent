Your Vexa keys

The bot runs on your own free Vexa account, so your meetings stay yours. Keys are stored encrypted.

  1. Sign in at vexa.ai with Google (new accounts get free credit).
  2. Account page → Create API Key → Bot Key (starts with vxa_bot_).
  3. Create another → Transcription Key (starts with vxa_tx_).

Report cards

Who gets the email after each meeting. Separate addresses with commas.

Your bot in the meeting

This is the name people see when the bot asks to join, and the hello it posts in the meeting chat once it is let in.

Leave the name and message blank to use the ones shown in grey. If you tell Telegram you're not joining, the bot says you couldn't join. A calendar you connected earlier keeps its old bot name until you disconnect and connect it again.

When you join the meeting yourself

Normally we ask on Telegram before each scheduled meeting, and the bot stays out when you say you're joining. Turn this on and the bot goes to every scheduled and calendar meeting anyway, quietly taking notes, and Telegram stops asking.

Before-meeting brief

About 10 minutes before each scheduled meeting, get a short note: what was decided last time, which tasks are still open and what is still unanswered. It comes on Telegram when connected, otherwise by email.

Telegram check-in
