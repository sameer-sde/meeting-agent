How it works

Before, during and after every meeting.

BEFORE

Walk in prepared

Ten minutes before the meeting you get a short brief: what was decided last time, which tasks are still open, and what is still unanswered.

DURING

The bot takes the notes

It joins as a visible participant with your name on it, says hello in the chat, and then only listens. Join yourself, or let it go in your place.

AFTER

Everything, in minutes

The report card arrives by email and on your dashboard, with the transcript, the recording, the tasks, and a chatbot you can question.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Is it free?

Meeting Agent itself is free. The bot runs on your own Vexa account, and every new Vexa account includes about 4 hours of meetings free. After that, Vexa charges roughly half a dollar per meeting hour, paid to Vexa directly.

Does it record without people knowing?

No. The bot is a visible participant with a name like "Alex's Notetaker", someone in the meeting has to admit it, and it posts a hello message in the chat. Whether recording is allowed depends on your company's rules and local law, so tell everyone first.

Do I have to be in the meeting?

No. That is the main idea: the bot attends in your place and you read the report card afterwards. You can also attend yourself and still let it take the notes.

Which meetings and languages does it support?

Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. It understands Hindi, Telugu and English, even mixed in one sentence, and writes the report in English. Zoom is not supported yet.

How do I start?

Create an account, confirm your email with the 6-digit code, then follow the Set up page: add two free keys from Vexa, paste a meeting link, and press Send bot. It takes about 5 minutes.

What can't it do?

It records audio, not video. It can't read messages typed in the meeting chat, and it only knows people who spoke or were invited, so someone who stayed silent won't show in attendance.
